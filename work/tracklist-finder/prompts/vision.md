Read the tracklist printed on this image of a sound carrier (CD, vinyl, cassette) exactly.
Rules:
- Take over only what is really on the image. Add nothing, fill in nothing from knowledge.
- Order and position labels exactly as printed (1, 2 … or A1, B1 … or side A/B).
- Duration only if printed; otherwise null.
- Mark hard-to-read entries with uncertain=true.
- Take over barcode digits, label and catalogue number if visible.
- Keep titles in their original language and spelling (do not translate).
- If no tracklist is visible: readable=false and tracks empty; describe in notes what is visible.
